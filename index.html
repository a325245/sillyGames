How to Play Santorini

Objective

Be the first player to move one of your Workers up onto the 3rd level of a building.

Setup

Players take turns placing two Workers each onto any empty space on the 5x5 board.

Turn Structure

On your turn, perform the following two steps in order:

  1. Move: Move one of your Workers into an adjacent space (up 1 level max, or down any levels).
  2. Build: Build a block (Level 1, 2, 3, or Dome) adjacent to the moved Worker.

Domes

When building on Level 3, a Dome is placed instead. Domes cap the building—no worker can move onto a dome.

Losing

If you cannot move any worker on your turn, you immediately lose!
