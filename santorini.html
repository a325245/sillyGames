How to Play Santorini

🎯 Objective

Be the first player to move one of your workers up onto Level 3 (the top stone block before a dome is built).

1. Setup Phase

Each player places 2 workers anywhere on empty ground tiles on the 5x5 grid.

2. Move Worker

Select one worker and move into an adjacent tile. You can move up max 1 level, but down any number of levels.

3. Build Block

Build a stone block or blue dome on an adjacent tile to the worker you just moved.

4. Domes & Blocking

Building on Level 3 creates a blue Dome. Domes cap tiles—no worker can move onto a dome!
