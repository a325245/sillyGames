Warhammer 40,000: Tacticus Rules

1. Official Damage & Pierce Formula

Every attack strikes N times (e.g., 6x Chain). Each hit deals:

Damage = Max(BaseDmg - Armor, BaseDmg × Pierce%)

Criticals roll sequentially per hit: if Hit 1 crits, Hit 2 can also crit!

2. Elevation & Terrain Cover

  • High Ground: Attacking a target on lower elevation grants +50% Damage.
  • Tall Grass / Smoke: Reduces incoming ranged hits by 2 (minimum 1 hit).
  • Trenches / Barricades: Reduces all incoming damage by 50%.
  • Fire Hexes: Deals burn damage at the start of the unit's turn.

3. Overwatch & Controls

Units with Overwatch (Certus & Imospekh) automatically fire upon the first enemy entering their attack range during the enemy turn. Toggle the Danger button to inspect enemy threat zones.

Touch/Mouse: Tap unit to select, tap blue hex to move, tap red hex to preview & confirm attack. Drag to orbit, pinch/scroll to zoom.
